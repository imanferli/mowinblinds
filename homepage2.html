Procurement FAQ

Does MOWIN handle B2B project volume orders?

Yes. MOWIN is a window blinds distributor for interior vendors, contractors, and corporations with tier pricing, custom sizing per opening, and phased installation across Jakarta, Bogor, Depok, Tangerang, and Bekasi.

How does distributor pricing work for vendors?

Pricing is calculated per opening based on actual measurements, fabric type, and manual or motorized system. Vendors receive exclusive tier pricing, ensuring margin for survey and installation services.

What is the SLA for survey and installation?

On-site measurement within 1–3 business days. Installation is scheduled per floor or zone, with night and weekend slots available for occupied offices, complete with formal handover documentation.
