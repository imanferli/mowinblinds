PROCUREMENT FAQ

Do you serve B2B volume and project orders?

Yes. MOWIN is a distributor supplying interior vendors, contractors and companies with tier pricing, per-opening custom sizing and phased installation across Jakarta, Bogor, Depok, Tangerang and Bekasi.

How does distributor pricing work for vendors?

Pricing is calculated per opening from actual dimensions, fabric type and manual or motorized systems. Trade partners receive a dedicated tier so margin remains for measure and fit services.

What is the survey and installation SLA?

Site surveys run within 1–3 working days. Installation is scheduled per floor or zone, with night and weekend slots for occupied offices, and closed with a signed handover report.

Can you match existing fabrics or branding?

Yes. Send a swatch, photo or RAL reference and we will match the closest project-grade fabric — including printed blinds with your client's logo or pattern.
